To strengthen your grip on these algorithms, you need to work on projects that challenge you with dirty real-world data, feature engineering, and model evaluation.
Here are four tiered projects designed to test and solidify your understanding of Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forests, and SVMs:
## 1. The Clean baseline: Housing Price Prediction

* The Goal: Predict the sale price of houses based on features like location, square footage, and year built.
* Why it strengthens your grip: This is the ultimate test for Linear Regression and Random Forest (Regression).
* Key Challenges: You will learn to handle missing data, handle skewed numerical values using log transformations, and use One-Hot Encoding on categorical data. You can directly compare how a simple linear model performs against a complex tree-based ensemble.
* Dataset Recommendation: Ames Housing Dataset or King County Housing Dataset (available on Kaggle).

## 2. The Classification Classic: Credit Card Fraud Detection

* The Goal: Classify bank transactions as either fraudulent or legitimate.
* Why it strengthens your grip: Perfect for Logistic Regression, Random Forest (Classifier), and SVM.
* Key Challenges: This dataset is highly imbalanced (99% of transactions are legitimate). Relying on basic "accuracy" will fail. It forces you to master critical evaluation metrics like Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and ROC-AUC, and experiment with handling imbalance (e.g., class weights or SMOTE).
* Dataset Recommendation: Credit Card Fraud Detection Dataset on Kaggle.

## 3. The Non-Linear Boundary Test: Customer Churn Prediction

* The Goal: Predict whether a telecom or SaaS customer will cancel their subscription based on usage patterns and account details.
* Why it strengthens your grip: Excellent for comparing Decision Trees against SVMs.
* Key Challenges: Trees will help you map out the decision rules clearly (interpretability), while SVMs will challenge you to understand Hyperparameter Tuning (tuning C, γ, and choosing the right kernel like RBF to find non-linear decision boundaries).
* Dataset Recommendation: Telco Customer Churn Dataset on Kaggle.

## 4. The Advanced Feature Challenge: Employee Attrition & Performance

* The Goal: Predict which employees are likely to leave a company and what factors drive their departure.
* Why it strengthens your grip: This project forces you to look inside the "black box" of Random Forests.
* Key Challenges: Beyond just predicting correctly, you will focus heavily on Feature Importance. You will learn to extract which variables (e.g., balance, monthly income, years at company) matter the most, transforming your model from a predictive tool into an actionable business insight tool.
* Dataset Recommendation: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance on Kaggle.

## Suggested Workflow for Any Project
To get the absolute most out of these projects, do not just plug the data into code. Follow this exact flow:

   1. EDA: Plot your data to find correlations and outliers.
   2. Baseline: Train a simple Logistic/Linear Regression first to get a baseline score.
   3. Complex Models: Train a Random Forest or SVM to see if it beats your baseline.
   4. Tune: Use GridSearchCV to dial in the hyperparameters.
